Risk Category Table
| Category              | Definition                                                              | Example                                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Regulation             | Risks from laws, compliance, or government/regulatory action            | "New AI regulations may..."                                                |
| Privacy                | Risks involving personal or confidential data                           | "AI systems process customer information..."                              |
| Cybersecurity          | AI-enabled attacks or security vulnerabilities                          | "Generative AI may increase phishing..."                                  |
| Intellectual Property  | Copyright, licensing, patent infringement                               | "AI-generated content may infringe..."                                    |
| Competitive            | Competitors' AI capabilities, failure to keep pace, market/execution risk | "Competitors may develop AI products superior to ours..."                 |
| Bias/Ethics            | Algorithmic bias, discrimination, fairness, ethical or societal concerns | "AI models may reflect biases included in the data on which they are trained..." |
| Accuracy/Reliability   | Hallucinations, factual errors, defects, flawed outputs                 | "AI-generated content may appear correct but be factually inaccurate..."  |
| Cost/Investment        | Capital/operating costs to build, deploy, or maintain AI                | "We will bear significant development and operational costs to build AI..." |
| Third-Party/Vendor     | Reliance on external AI providers, partners, or third-party training data | "This AI may be developed by Microsoft or others, including OpenAI..."   |
| Geopolitical/Trade     | Export controls, government restrictions on AI products/technology      | "Export controls targeting GPUs and semiconductors associated with AI..." |
| Other                  | Mentions AI but no clear risk, or doesn't fit above categories          | "We use AI to improve customer service..."                                |

Notes on applying categories:
- Many passages plausibly touch more than one category (e.g., a passage about AI output that is biased, leaks confidential data, and infringes IP touches Bias/Ethics, Privacy, and Intellectual Property at once). Assign the single most prominent/leading category rather than splitting the row.
- "Other" is also used for passages that mention AI/automation-adjacent terms but do not actually describe AI (see Quality = 0 below), and for purely descriptive statements (e.g., "We are building AI into our products") that don't state a risk.

Disclosure Quality Table
| Score | Meaning                                           | Notes                                                                 |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0     | Not actually about AI (false positive)             | Passage was keyword-matched (e.g., "automation") but doesn't reference AI/ML at all |
| 1     | Generic boilerplate                                | Mentions AI but is descriptive, a list item, or a vague/general risk statement with no concrete mechanism or impact |
| 2     | Moderately specific                                | Describes a specific risk or mechanism, but without a quantified or clearly named concrete detail (e.g., named law, dollar impact, specific attack type) |
| 3     | Highly specific with concrete details or examples  | Names a specific law/regulation (e.g., EU AI Act, GDPR), a quantified impact (e.g., "fines up to 7% of revenue"), a named third party (e.g., OpenAI), or a concrete mechanism of harm |
